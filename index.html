How to play

  • ↑/W/Space accelerate · ↓/S brake · ←→/AD steer
  • P/Esc pause · M sound on/off · H high contrast · I announce status (screen readers)
  • Ride over the glowing blue lightning orbs to boost: your bike's limit goes up by 20 km/h (a standard 100 km/h bike reaches 120) for a few seconds, depending on the bike. Boosts stack. Rivals grab them too, and an orb comes back a few seconds after someone takes it.
  • Garage: pick from 10 bike types (scooter up to superbike) and a colour. Each bike has its own top speed, acceleration, handling, boost time and off-road grip.
  • Race types: a 15 km sprint, or a circuit with 1, 3, 5 or 10 laps (2.4 km per lap). Lap times are shown at the end. Traffic: overtake the cars, buses and autos. Hitting one slows you down.
  • Stay on the tarmac. Grass, sand and kerbs slow you to 45 km/h, and hitting trees, signs or buildings nearly stops you.
  • Bumping into a rider from behind slows you down, so steer round them to overtake.
  • Auto-steer assist steers for you (lines up boosts and avoids riders). You handle the throttle. One-button mode steers for you too: hold any key or the big button to go. Add Auto-accelerate and you don't need to press anything.
  • Time scale: speeds and distances are real (15 km, km/h), but game time runs 3× faster than real time, so a full 15 km race at 100 km/h shows about 9:00 on the race clock and takes about 3 minutes. You can slow everything further in Settings (game speed).
